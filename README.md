# Cougar-Nation
It's like the backrooms but with APs

## Crafting
- Crafting grid is **3x3**.
- 1 stool -> 2 wooden planks.
- 9 screws -> 1 iron ingot.
- 3 iron ingots + 2 wooden planks -> 1 axe.
- 1 rubber duck, trash bin, or water bottle -> 1 plastic.
- 1 backpack, pair of shoes, or red ribbon -> 1 fabric.
- 1 fabric + 1 screw + 7 plastic -> 1 hall pass.

## Doors
- Select a hall pass in the hotbar to open bathroom doors.
- Classroom doors start boarded up. Select an axe and click the door three times to chop away the boards, then click again to open it.
- Every classroom has a teacher who offers a math test when you enter. Decline to step back outside and try later; pass at least 6 of 8 addition, subtraction, multiplication, and long-division problems to earn a Teacher ID Pass. Each classroom can be passed once. The teacher's red vision is dangerous while your phone is out, so use its calculator carefully.
- Use the Teacher ID Pass as a key on the door at the back of the starting cell to enter the Pool Rooms.

## Fishing
- Craft a **Fishing Rod** from 1 Screw + 5 Fabric + 3 Wooden Planks (shapeless, in the crafting grid).
- Ponds are scattered through the halls (outside the classrooms and bathrooms). Hold the rod, look at a pond and click to cast. When the bobber dips and the prompt says it's biting, click quickly to reel in; too early or too late and you catch nothing.
- Fish and sell prices (sell them in Treemu): **Bluegill** (common) $5, **Bass** (uncommon) $14, **Golden Koi** (rare) $45. The rod itself sells for about $48.

## Treemu (phone shop)
- Open the phone (F) and tap Treemu. You start with $20.
- **Sell** any inventory item except the phone and Teacher ID Pass. Crafted items are worth their ingredients plus 50%, so harder recipes sell for more (e.g. Iron Ingot $14, Axe $69, Hall Pass $51).
- **Buy** the simple items found on the ground (stool, plank, screws, duck, bin, bottle, backpack, shoes, ribbon) and Iron Ingots at about double their sell value.
- After a purchase it shows "packages delivering..."; a drone flies through the halls to you and drops the package at your feet. Click it to collect the items.

## Stocks (phone app)
- Open Stocks on the phone to trade with your Treemu cash. Pick one of five stocks, view its live price graph, and buy or sell shares. Prices update every ~1.5 seconds.
- **Corbok Coin** never goes down but grows slowly. **Dog Coin** and **Crapto Coin** are volatile (Crapto can crash hard). **Public Schools** trends down and falls often. **Treemu** grows faster the more you buy from the Treemu shop.

## Character look
- Open Locker from the main menu to build a customizable 3D character: preset, outfit, jacket color, skin tone, hair style/color, pants, and build. Drag the preview to rotate it.
- Save the look (stored in the browser). Bathroom mirrors are real reflections of the whole room (walls, stalls, doors, monsters) and show your 3D character, mirroring your movement. The shared model code is in `assets/avatar.js`.
